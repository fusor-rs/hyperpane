# hyperpane identity

hyperpane follows [fusor's identity](https://github.com/fusor-rs/fusor/blob/main/apps/landing/BRANDING.md),
as hypercmd does: the same palette, wordmark typeface and two-piece
construction.

The mark is a window in two pieces: a frame, the top bar and left edge of a
window, and the pane inside it. The frame's two ends and the pane's corner are
cut on the same diagonal, echoing the diagonal cuts of fusor's F, and the gap
between the pieces is constant. The wordmark is Lexend SemiBold, outlined to
paths. All logo SVGs contain vector paths, with no embedded raster images,
external fonts, or scripts.

| Use | Artwork |
| --- | --- |
| Symbol in color | [hyperpane.svg](hyperpane.svg) |
| Horizontal logo | [Light backgrounds](hyperpane-horizontal.svg), [dark backgrounds](hyperpane-horizontal-dark.svg) |

Use the symbol alone for small icons and avatars. Use the dark-background
horizontal logo where the page background is dark; it keeps the symbol in
color and switches the wordmark to warm white.

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| Frame | Vermilion `#DF5036` |
| Pane | Oxide `#A83228` |
| Wordmark on dark backgrounds | Warm white `#FAF9F6` |
| Wordmark | Charcoal `#272A2C` |

Preserve the proportions, the gap between the two pieces, and clear space.
